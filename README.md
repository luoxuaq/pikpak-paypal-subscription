## PikPak Premium Plans: Full Breakdown

PikPak's pricing structure has one thing that trips people up: there are two tiers of Premium — Global and Regional — and the system assigns you to one automatically at checkout. Understanding this before you pay saves confusion.

### Global Premium vs. Regional Premium

**Global Premium** applies to users in 33 countries and regions, including the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Israel, Turkey, and others. Global Premium users get download speeds up to 20MB/s per connection, with no speed throttling regardless of where in the world they travel.

**Regional Premium** applies to everyone outside that list. You get the same 10TB storage and all the same core features — cloud downloading, 4K streaming, Telegram bot, browser extension, cloud decompression, WebDAV — but download speed is capped at up to 8MB/s per connection. If you travel to a Global Premium country, your speeds may be limited and PikPak may prompt you to upgrade. Regional Premium is priced lower to reflect service cost differences across markets.

One important note: redemption codes are tier-specific. A Regional Premium code won't activate in a Global Premium country. If you ever buy a third-party code, verify which tier it covers.

## What If Your PayPal Payment Fails?

PayPal payments don't always go through on the first try. Here's how to handle it if yours doesn't:

**Common reasons PayPal payments fail for PikPak:**

- Your PayPal account has insufficient balance or a linked bank account that couldn't fund the transaction
- Your PayPal account has spending limits or security holds on new merchant transactions
- Your region's PayPal setup has restrictions on certain types of subscriptions
- Temporary connectivity issue between PikPak's payment processor and PayPal

**What to do:**

1. **Try the web checkout directly** — `mypikpak.com/drive/payment` — rather than paying through the mobile app, as web checkout can sometimes bypass payment routing issues that affect in-app purchases
2. **Switch payment methods temporarily** — if PayPal is failing, PikPak's crypto payment option is often the most universally reliable alternative; credit card is the other obvious fallback
3. **Check your PayPal account** — log into PayPal independently and confirm there are no pending verifications, account limitations, or unresolved disputes that might be blocking outgoing payments to new merchants
4. **Contact PikPak support** — if the payment went through on PayPal's side but Premium hasn't appeared after one hour, email `support@mypikpak.com` with a screenshot of the PayPal transaction and your PikPak account email

Premium benefits usually appear within minutes of a successful payment. PikPak's stated window is up to one hour.

## How to Cancel PikPak Premium Purchased via PayPal

If you subscribed through PayPal's auto-renewal system and want to stop future charges, you have two places to do it:

**Through PikPak:**
Go to the PikPak website, log in, and navigate to **Manage Subscription** or **Purchase History** on the payment page. You should see an option to cancel the auto-renewal there.

**Through PayPal directly:**
1. Log in to your PayPal account
2. Go to **Settings** → **Payments** → **Automatic Payments** (or **Subscriptions** in the PayPal app)
3. Find PikPak in the list of merchants
4. Cancel the automatic payment from there

Your Premium benefits continue until the end of the current billing period you've already paid for — canceling doesn't create an immediate refund or cutoff, it just stops the next renewal from being charged.
